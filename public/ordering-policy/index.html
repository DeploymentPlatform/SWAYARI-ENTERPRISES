Operational Guidelines

Ordering, Quotations & Delivery Policy

Catalogue & Request Model:
“Our team will contact you to confirm availability, final pricing, shipping charges and delivery details. Submitting this request does not confirm a purchase or reserve stock.”

1. The Order Request Process

SWAYARI ENTERPRISES operates as a bespoke catalogue and personal consultation atelier. We do not collect credit cards, debit cards, or process instant automated transactions on this website.

  1. Browsing: You are free to browse products, read specifications, and inspect photos without signing in.
  2. Adding to Request: Clicking "Add to Request" initiates a Google sign-in to associate items with your account.
  3. Submitting Request: Enter your contact telephone number and delivery address to submit your request list.
  4. Reference Generated: You receive a unique reference code (e.g. SW-YYYYMMDD-XXXX) which is recorded in our cloud system and visible in your order history.

2. Telephone Consultation & Customisation

Within one business day of your submission, a SWAYARI coordinator will call your registered contact number. During this consultation, we confirm:

  • Handcrafted piece availability or artisan production lead time.
  • Custom text for brass dedication plaques or celebratory cards.
  • Presentation packaging (standard vs velvet keepsake box).
  • Exact destination accessibility and delivery timeframe.

3. Formal Quotation & Shipping Charges

Because our products are crafted from authentic metal alloys, silver finishes, and solid framing, shipping charges are calculated strictly based on weight, dimensions, fragile protective crating, and transit insurance to your specific destination pincode. We do not charge arbitrary flat fees.

Our team issues a formal Quotation Revision in your account dashboard displaying item prices, verified shipping charges, and applicable taxes. Only after you approve this quotation is your order confirmed.

4. Packing, Dispatch & Transit

All items undergo individual quality verification prior to dispatch. Fragile devotional plaques and brass urulis are packed in multilayer shock-absorbent foam and reinforced outer containers. Tracked courier airway bills (AWB) are shared once dispatched.

5. Contacting Our Team

For questions regarding any pending order request or bulk requirement, email us at swayarienterprises@gmail.com.

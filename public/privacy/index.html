Privacy Policy

SWAYARI ENTERPRISES ("SWAYARI", "we", "us", or "our") respects your personal privacy. This policy explains how we handle information collected when you browse our catalogue or submit order requests.

1. Information We Collect

We believe in minimal data collection. We only gather information strictly necessary to fulfill order requests and consultation:

  • Google Identity Profile: When you sign in using Google Identity Services, we receive basic verified identity attributes: your Google subject identifier (sub), verified email address, profile display name, and avatar picture. We do not access your Google contacts, Google Drive, or any other Google account services.
  • Order Request Information: When submitting an order request, you provide your contact telephone number, shipping street address, city, state, pincode, and optional customisation instructions.
  • Technical Information: Standard server logs that record IP addresses and request timestamps to defend against abuse and ensure platform stability.

2. How Information is Used

Your information is used solely for the following business purposes:

  • Associating requested items and quotation histories with your account.
  • Allowing our customer coordinators to contact you via telephone or WhatsApp regarding your order specifications.
  • Calculating accurate, insured courier shipping charges to your address.
  • Maintaining secure authenticated customer sessions.

3. Data Security & Storage

All data is maintained within Cloudflare's secure global infrastructure (Cloudflare D1). Session identifiers are stored exclusively in HttpOnly, SameSite=Lax, encrypted cookies to prevent client-side script interception (XSS). We never store authentication tokens in localStorage.

4. No Sale or Third-Party Marketing

We never sell, rent, or trade your contact information, order details, or telephone numbers to advertising networks, marketers, or data brokers. Information is shared only with verified courier partners (e.g. Bluedart, Delhivery, India Post) strictly to carry out delivery of confirmed orders.

5. Inquiries & Deletion Requests

If you wish to review, update, or request deletion of your account and past order requests, please email us directly from your registered Google email at swayarienterprises@gmail.com.
